# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Oceanographers & Marine Researchers:** Conduct telemetry analysis, hydrodynamic Monte Carlo drift simulations, and evaluate XGBoost risk models.
2. **Coastal & Municipal Authorities (Operations):** Monitor real-time coastal hotspot risks, dispatch clean-up fleets via Hungarian optimization, and oversee zone status.
3. **Field Cleanup Teams & Vessel Operators:** Execute on-ground recovery missions, monitor real-time debris detection (YOLO/OceanEye), and track circular economy valuation/recycling streams.

## Product Purpose

TIDAL is an intelligent, physics-informed marine debris telemetry, drift forecasting, and automated clean-up dispatch platform focusing on coastal environments (currently Mumbai Coast). It bridges raw oceanographic/weather sensor data, machine learning predictions (XGBoost, YOLOv8/11), and automated fleet logistics to maximize circular recovery of ocean plastics.

## Positioning

Unlike static map visualizers or manual clean-up spreadsheets, TIDAL combines real-time hydrodynamic physics modeling (Monte Carlo particle drift), machine-learning beaching forecasts with explainable SHAP factors, live underwater computer vision analysis, and automated Hungarian-algorithm fleet assignment in a unified operational intelligence interface.

## Operating Context

High-density coastal monitoring command center, maritime field operations, and scientific research workstations. Needs high scanability, rapid anomaly detection, high-contrast data visualization (telemetry, maps, 3D simulations), and clear status indicators under various operating lighting conditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Preserve:** All existing routes (`/overview`, `/simulate`, `/hotspots`, `/circular-recovery`, `/model-lab`, `/dashboard`), navigation flow, data schemas, WebSocket connections (`ws://localhost:8000/ws/live`), backend REST endpoints, and state management (Zustand).
- **Redesign Scope:** Front-end presentation, UI/UX, typography, layout hierarchy, color palette execution, micro-interactions, responsive states (desktop to mobile), and visual polish.
- **Tone:** Technical, authoritative, sleek, oceanic-tactical, high-end scientific intelligence. Avoid generic AI SaaS tropes (e.g., generic purple gradients, bubbly rounded cards with weak contrast).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** TIDAL
- **Identity:** Marine intelligence, oceanographic research, tactical maritime command, precision scientific tools.
- **Palette Anchor:** Deep oceanic abyssal dark grounds, crisp bioluminescent cyan/teal accents, precise maritime alerts (amber, coral, emerald).

## Product Principles

1. **Information Density with Absolute Clarity:** Enable deep scientific scanning without visual clutter.
2. **Tactical Precision:** Data, telemetry, and metrics should feel instrument-grade and dependable.
3. **Dynamic Oceanic Energy:** Purposeful, fluid motion and crisp spatial depth that reflects the marine environment.
4. **Resilient Responsive Operation:** Full functional utility whether viewed on a dual-monitor command display or a field tablet/phone.
